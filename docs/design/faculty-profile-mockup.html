Areas of Expertise: Distributed Systems, Machine Learning, Human-Computer Interaction, Cloud Computing, Data Privacy

Profile

Dr. Anjali Mehta completed her PhD in Computer Science at IIT Bombay, specializing in distributed systems and large-scale data infrastructure. She has over 14 years of combined academic and industry experience, having previously worked as a Senior Engineer at a cloud infrastructure firm before transitioning to full-time academia. Her current research focuses on privacy-preserving machine learning and resilient distributed architectures.
